OUR CORE PRINCIPLES

Built for academic accuracy and student trust.

🛡️

100% Grounded Academic Integrity

We enforce strict prompt constraints. The AI is forbidden from inventing facts or using external opinions. Every quiz answer, summary point, and flashcard is strictly grounded in your uploaded text.

🧠

Active Recall by Design

Passive reading yields less than 20% retention after one week. By combining automated quiz generation with 3D flashcard retrieval, NoteWise AI forces active cognitive recall for long-term mastery.

🔒

Student Privacy & Document Isolation

Your notes belong to you. Study documents are strictly isolated to your user account, stored in a private local database, and never used to train public language models.

⚡

High-Availability Multi-Model Architecture

Powered by Google Gemini GenAI SDK with automated exponential backoff retries and dynamic multi-model fallback cascades, ensuring your study sessions stay uninterrupted even during peak traffic.
