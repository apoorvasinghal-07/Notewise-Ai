Q: Explain Round Robin Scheduling in Operating Systems.

1. Definition: Preemptive scheduling algorithm designed specifically for time-sharing systems.
2. Time Quantum: Each process receives a fixed time slice (quantum). If unfinished, it is preempted to the back of the ready queue.
3. Starvation-Free: Fair CPU allocation ensures zero starvation.
4. Trade-off: Too small a quantum causes excessive context-switch overhead; too large degrades into FCFS.
